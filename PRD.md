# Personal Portfolio Website

## Project Overview

**Purpose:** Create a professional, skills-focused portfolio website that establishes credibility, drives social connections, and builds trust with recruiters and potential collaborators.

**Target Audience:**

- Technical recruiters
- Hiring managers
- Potential collaborators and peers
- Professional network connections

---

## Core Objectives

1. **Establish Professional Presence:** Create a memorable first impression through sophisticated design
2. **Showcase Skills:** Highlight technical capabilities and expertise without relying heavily on work experience
3. **Enable Connection:** Make it easy for visitors to reach out and connect
4. **Build Trust:** Demonstrate professionalism and attention to detail through design quality

---

## Design Requirements

### Visual Identity

**Theme:** Dark Mode Only

**Color Palette - "Modern & High-End":**

- **Primary:** Black - Background, primary text
- **Secondary:** White - Text, accents, contrast elements
- **Accent:** Gold - Highlights, CTAs, interactive elements, section dividers

**Typography:**

- Suggested: Clean, modern sans-serif fonts
- Hierarchy: Clear distinction between headings, subheadings, and body text

**Design Principles:**

- Minimalist and sophisticated
- High contrast for readability
- Generous white space
- Subtle animations and transitions
- Premium, luxury aesthetic

### Responsiveness

- **Mobile-first approach**
- Breakpoints: Mobile, Desktop
- Optimized loading for all devices

---

## Key Sections & Features

### Milestone 1: Hero Section - Business Card Concept

**Design Concept:** Digital business card that serves as the first impression

---

### Milestone 2: Skills Showcase Section

**Purpose:** Comprehensive display of technical capabilities

**Content Structure:**

- **Technical Skills:**
  - Programming languages (with proficiency indicators)
  - Frameworks & libraries
  - Tools & platforms
  - Databases & cloud services
- **Soft Skills:**
  - Problem-solving
  - Collaboration
  - Learning agility
  - Communication

**Display Options:**

- **Grid with icons:** Visual grid of technology logos/icons
- **Tag cloud:** Dynamic, hoverable skill tags

---

### Milestone 3: About/Story Section

**Purpose:** Humanize your profile and explain your journey

**Content Suggestions:**

- Your background and what drives you
- Current learning focus or goals
- What you're passionate about in tech
- Campus club involvement (presented as leadership/learning experience)
- Your approach to problem-solving

**Visual Treatment:**

- Clean paragraph format or short story-like blocks
- Optional: Timeline of your learning journey
- Gold divider lines between sections
- Personal interests or hobbies (to show personality)

**Questions to Clarify:**

- What's the key message you want to convey about yourself?
- Should we highlight your campus club experience? If yes, what was your role?
- Any particular achievements or moments you're proud of?

---

### Milestone 4: Contact & Social Connection Section

**Purpose:** Make it effortless for visitors to connect

**Required Elements:**

- **Primary contact options:**
  - Email (with copy-to-clipboard functionality)
  - LinkedIn profile link
  - GitHub profile link
- **Optional social links:**
  - Twitter/X
  - Medium/Blog
  - Other relevant platforms

**Design:**

- Gold-bordered contact card or simple contact form
- Social icons with gold hover effects
- "Let's Connect" CTA headline
- Optional: Availability status (e.g., "Open to opportunities")

**Functionality:**

- Click to copy email
- Direct links open in new tabs
- Smooth transitions and micro-interactions

**Questions to Clarify:**

- Do you want a contact form or just direct links?
- Which social platforms should be included?
- Any specific call-to-action text you prefer?

---

## Technical Requirements

### Technology Stack

**Frontend:**

- **Framework:** NextJS
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS or styled-components
- **Animations:** Framer Motion or CSS animations
- **Icons:** Lucide React

**Performance:**

- Lighthouse score > 90
- Lazy loading where appropriate
- Fast initial load time (< 2 seconds)

### Principles

- DRY - Whenever a repitive code is present, a component should be preferred.
- It should as static as possible. Thus, prefer client components only when needed.

### Assumptions

- Any details that are known yet should have a have placeholder.
